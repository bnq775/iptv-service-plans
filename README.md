## How should you test the “most reliable IPTV service” claim?

This is the part worth doing instead of simply trusting a channel count.

Start with the channels you actually watch. Open several live channels rather than testing one random station for 30 seconds.

Then test during your normal viewing period. Evening and live-event traffic are much more useful for a reliability check than a quiet afternoon.

Next, test the device you actually own. A service can behave differently depending on the television, streaming stick, Wi-Fi setup and player app.

Also check the EPG. A huge channel list is less impressive when you still have to guess what is playing.

Finally, test support before there is a crisis. TiviLive says its live chat is available 24/7, and its FAQ also lists WhatsApp and Telegram as support channels.

Your own internet connection matters too. TiviLive recommends at least 10 Mbps for HD streaming and 25 Mbps for 4K in its terms, while its installation guide recommends Ethernet when Wi-Fi instability is causing buffering.
